Coinbase Crypto Prob Scanner v4.20.1

Fixes objective-semantics wiring regression from v4.20.0.

What changed
- Objective semantics are now attached to every scored row via scanner._score_band(), so visible, suppressed, blocked, replay, and review-pack rows populate:
  objective_score_band
  objective_score_band_label
  objective_quality_reference_rate
  objective distance/floor fields
- Decision summaries now mention confirmed/strong/priority/elite edge counts even when validated-band rows are also present.
- Model output distribution headlines are now aware of the replay-backed raw-score-baseline contract, avoiding the misleading default claim that Stage 2 compression is the leading hypothesis when the baseline already says semantics are the blocker.
- Live selection logic remains unchanged. Threshold remains 0.35.
