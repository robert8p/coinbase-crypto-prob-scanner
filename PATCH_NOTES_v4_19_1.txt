Coinbase Crypto Prob Scanner v4.19.1

Hotfix purpose
- Fix Raw Score Baseline Lab runtime failure introduced in v4.19.0.

Root cause
- The replay service was partially wired for capture_full_rankable_rows, but the new flag was not threaded through _execute_replay_window and _run_replay_scan.
- Result: the UI baseline run failed immediately with an unexpected keyword argument error instead of producing the offline baseline pack.

What changed
- Added capture_full_rankable_rows to HistoricalReplayService._execute_replay_window.
- Added capture_full_rankable_rows to HistoricalReplayService._run_replay_scan.
- Aggregated captured_rankable_rows across replay timestamps so RawScoreBaselineService receives the full resolved stage-2 rankable set it expects.
- Bumped version to 4.19.1.

Live-path impact
- No intended change to live selection semantics.
- Legacy remains the effective live selection engine.
- Stage 1 remains stage1_opportunity_model.
- Effective live raw threshold remains 0.35.
